## Equation convention (invariant)

`ħ²ψ″ = Q(z)ψ`, with `Q(z) = V(z) − E` - the Iwaki–Nakanishi normalization. Every
turning point, period, Voros symbol and Stokes line is defined against this `Q`.

## Key invariants

- **Immutability**: every operation returns a new object; constructors copy input
  vectors; invalid operations throw typed `ExactWKBError`s.
- **No AbstractAlgebra in the public API**: ring elements are confined to the WKB
  recursion internals (`RiccatiTerm` numerators live in an AA `polynomial_ring`).
  Public accessors return plain `Number`s and `Resurgence.FormalSeries`.
- **Precision from the caller**: exact (`Rational`/`Int`) Q flows to `BigFloat` at the
  caller's `setprecision`; Float64 contours/tracers give Float64 results.
- **√Q branch tracking is the top correctness risk** - mitigated by (i) the
  augmented-state tracer (`w = √Q` is a state variable, no discrete sign choices),
  (ii) node-reference continuity in quadrature, (iii) orientation-pinning oracles
  (`∮√(z²−E) = ∓iπE`, Airy three-rays).
- **The reduction `S_even = −½ ∂log S_odd`** is an enforced oracle
  (`even_odd_residual ≈ 0`), never used to compute - it justifies working with
  `∮S_odd` alone.
- **The DDP convention ledger** (header of `src/ddp.jl`, every item pinned by an
  oracle in `test/test_ddp.jl`): (1) lateral-sum signs are Resurgence's
  (disc = s⁺ − s⁻); (2) `s₊(V_γ) = s₋(V_γ)(1 + V_{γ'})^{⟨γ,γ'⟩}` with the wall
  symbol median-summed - the Stokes constant is the exact integer `⟨γ,γ'⟩`;
  (3) `⟨γ,γ'⟩` = signed same-sheet crossing count of canonical lifts (principal √Q
  branch at the first vertex, the same rule as `period_integral`); (4) the wall
  symbol must decay along the wall (`Re(Z e^{-iθ_c}) < 0`), enforced by a typed
  error, never auto-flipped; (5) cluster dictionary `y_j = V_{γ_j}` with exchange
  matrix `B = −P` (`b_kj = ⟨γ_j,γ_k⟩`), wall-crossing = FZ y-mutation `μ_k`;
  (6) MGS charge order = the order walls are crossed as θ **decreases from the
  chamber's own θ₀**, i.e. increasing `mod(θ₀ − θ_c, π)`; the plain θ-decreasing form
  is its θ₀ → π⁻ (top-chamber) case. Voros symbols never move -
  crossing θ_c only rotates the Laplace ray in the Borel plane.
- **The signed frame (M4b, 2026-07-19 - headers of `signed_frame.jl`,
  `charge_lattice.jl`, `bps.jl`)**: the decay rule of item 4 fixes a *representative*
  of ±γ_e, not the physical charge. The physical cycle is `γ_e^phys = ε_e·γ_e`, with
  ε solved from the **tightened keystone** `P == −εBε` (M4's `|P| == |B|` was the
  slack that hid the layer). Item 5 then applies verbatim to `signed_pairing`, and
  `contours`/`central_charges` stay in the decay frame so item 4 and the M5
  orientation ledger are untouched. Solvability needs the sign cocycle to close
  around every cycle of the quiver graph - vacuous on tree chambers, a genuine
  condition on *cyclic* ones (the case M4 refused). **Every chamber is now
  supported**, and `_matches_saddles` is a verification of a frame correct by
  construction rather than a filter for choosing one.
  ε is *chamber-local*: it is not transported and is not continuous across a wall
  even where the cycle persists; the residual gauge (one global sign per component)
  is physically inert. `flip` is *handed* (`direction = ±1`).
- **Graph topology is Float64 by design** even for exact/BigFloat problems - a
  Stokes graph and its triangulation are discrete data; `charge_basis` periods then
  follow the caller's precision.

## The Riccati representation

`S_{-1} = √Q`, `S_0 = −Q′/(4Q)`, `S_{m+1} = −(Σ_{j} S_j S_{m-j} + S_m′)/(2S_{-1})`.
Every `S_m` is exactly `p_m(z)·u^{ε_m}/Q^{k_m}` with `u = √Q` and `ε_m = parity(m)`.
Storing `(p_m, k_m, ε_m)` as a `RiccatiTerm` avoids any rational-function field, so the
BigFloat coefficient ring stays gcd-free and numerically safe. `arithmetic =
:auto/:exact/:bigfloat` chooses the numerator ring (`QQ` vs `RealField`); exact blow-up
is the known cost driver (Resurgence M8 fast kernels are the planned relief).
